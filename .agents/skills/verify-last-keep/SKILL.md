# Verify Last Keep

This is the definition of done. `mix test` does not replace it. A passing run that only counts meshes, or that never clicks, is not done. Run the Playwright suite against the server you changed. After a deploy, run the same suite with `BASE_URL` set to the live origin.

The checks live in `e2e/scenarios.js`. `e2e/coop.spec.js` calls those functions and does not restate them. When a check changes, change `e2e/scenarios.js`. Do not copy the steps into this skill.

How roles touch is `docs/GAME-INTERACTIONS.md`. `mix test` includes `test/last_keep/game/interactions_test.exs`, which checks `docs/interactions.json` against the simulation. A playtest that reviews roles uses `.agents/skills/playtest-last-keep/SKILL.md` and does not replace this browser proof.

## Launch

From the repo root, with Elixir 1.18 and OTP 28 on `PATH`:

```sh
mix setup
npm ci
npm ci --prefix assets
npx playwright install chromium
PORT=4000 mix phx.server
```

Ready means `curl -fsS http://127.0.0.1:4000/` contains `Last Keep` and the body contains `Create game`. A socket that accepts TCP is not ready.

`npx playwright test` starts that server itself when `BASE_URL` is unset and port 4000 is free. If a server you started is already on port 4000, the suite reuses it. Restart that server after a code change or the browser will prove the old build. The shell often exports `BASE_URL` for production. Unset it for a local run.

The viewport is 1280 by 800. That is the size a manual playtest uses. Do not shrink it to make a sprite fit.

## Doctor

```sh
curl -fsS "${BASE_URL:-http://127.0.0.1:4000}/" | grep -q "Create game"
```

Stop if that fails. Do not drive a server this run did not start, except the production origin after a deploy of the commit you are proving.

## Drive

```sh
npx playwright test
```

Against a deployed site:

```sh
BASE_URL=https://agentyard.letz.tw npx playwright test
```

`e2e/coop.spec.js` runs sixteen scenarios exported from `e2e/scenarios.js`. `e2e/mobile.spec.js` runs `mobileTouch` and `phoneClear` on a landscape phone with touch:

- `twoPlayerNight` — Bob the warrior hosts, LLL the mage joins from the invite query. The night waits. The yard light is `day` while the party waits, and `night` after the host starts. The waiting yard calls `yardGrade`, one framebuffer read for mood and ground. A flat field (contrast under 9), a lime share over 0.08, or a near-ground spread under 8 fails. The framebuffer is flipped to top-down before that grade, so the band is the near yard. A bottom-up read scores the far wash. The light changes first, and the grade runs after, so the read does not stall the page through the dusk window. After the host starts, dusk must pass the same comparison: contrast at least 9, cool wash at most 0.25, warm tail at least 0.03. `duskGrade` owns the keep stone, the pine, and the plate. Keep stone and the pine wall stay at least 25 degrees of hue apart, and the hero plate stays under 0.2 saturation. `sampleObject` renders that mesh or sprite alone and `maskedStats` takes the median of its own pixels. Fewer than 24 throws. The plate sample is the steel on the 32-unit card at height 18. Height 40 is above that card. The dusk wait stays until the height-18 median is under 0.2, because a dark miss divides by a tiny red and the ratio jumps past 1. `nightGrade` grades the night ground from that same single framebuffer read. Dusk sunset flecks stay out of the pine median, because the sample is the crown's own pixels and a gap or a fleck is not the median. Night has to pass before the fight. A golf-course day or a lavender dusk fails. The pine wall count on `forestLook.wall` is at least 80, and the seventy outer stumps and felled logs stay woodless. The score chip reads `Score 0`. A click that only focuses the window does not swing. A window blur that leaves the document focused keeps the swing flag, so the next click is not thrown away. A click with no building armed does not spend wood. An illegal wall says why, spends nothing, and the reason hides. One click after the window blurs places a legal wall, spends wood, and disarms. Arming a wall draws a ground ring whose radius is that hero's reach. Movement while walking interpolates. The host's start plays the horn and the night loop. An edge call does not cover the roster. Both clients see goblins on the yard, and the two bodies are drawn apart. The mage's Fireball is cast when the night starts, while that hero is still up. The hold waits until the guest is visible and its tick is advancing at the live rate, and the shot still has to appear within 5 seconds. Damage numbers rise and fade, and `Slain` is on screen. The wave label only moves forward, from `Wave 1` to `Wave 2`. The keep survives. The score chip matches the snapshot. The cue list has the horn, the sting, a hit, a kill, and the build. The same bus reports the fall. Mute and volume survive a reload. Forge, Spire, and relic copy are on the HUD, and `window.__lastKeep.smoothing` is true. The wave line, relic lines, resource tally, roster, ability keys, and build keys are captions: `assertDiegeticHud` fails an opaque fill, a thick border, or a combined box at the old panel area. The yard base is the concept clearing tile, and the keep stone wears the masonry crop. `tiles.test.js` rebuilds each phase from `theme.jpg` and fails a pixel more than distance 1 from that phase's palette, a block p25 under 8, or a hard edge under 0.05. The night tile is steps 0.50 and 0.40. Collapsing those greens to two colors drops block p25 under 8.

## Evidence

Write screenshots to `tmp/verify-artifacts/`. `twoPlayerNight` writes `coop-host.png`, `coop-guest.png`, `reason-host.png`, `wall-host.png`, `wave-host.png`, `wave-guest.png`, `combat-host.png`, and `cleared-host.png`. `joinFromForm` writes `form-join.png`. `heroSilhouettes` writes `heroes-host.png`. `mobileTouch` writes `mobile-landscape.png` and `mobile-portrait.png`. Cleanup does not delete that directory.

A passing run shows both players, a placed wall, the refusal reason, goblins on the yard inside the camera, a damage number, and a cleared wave. A roster assertion without the screenshot is not proof. A monster count without red pixels at the goblin's projected position is not proof.

## Cleanup

Stop the `mix phx.server` process you started. Do not kill every `beam` on the machine. Leave `tmp/verify-artifacts/` in place.

## Helpers
